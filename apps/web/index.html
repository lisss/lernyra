How to turn a job title into a study plan

Lernyra is a free planner for role requirements and skill gaps. It does not list job openings.

  1. Choose a role, such as Software Engineer, Python Developer, or Data Engineer.
  2. Add skills you already have, or upload a CV so matching skills are filled in.
  3. Read the gaps, then open the books, courses, certifications, and interview prep lined up for them.

Questions people ask

How do I see what skills a job needs?

Pick a title on Lernyra, or open its role guide. Each guide lists the skills that title usually requires, with the core skills called out first.

How does Lernyra build a learning path?

Add the skills you already have, or upload a CV. Lernyra compares them with the role and lines up books, courses, certifications, and interview prep for the gaps.

Can I compare a role with my CV?

Yes. Upload a PDF or text CV, or paste skills. Lernyra keeps the skills that match the role you picked and uses the gaps for the study plan.

Which job titles have a guide?

There is a guide for common software titles, including Software Engineer, Python Developer, Frontend Engineer, Data Engineer, DevOps Engineer, and Machine Learning Engineer. The full list is on the role guides page.
